Irregular Verbs across Languages

This is a list of all meanings with which in some language an irregular verb exists.

Inclusion criteria:

  1. uninflected (incl. derivational inflection)
  2. irregular (excl. purely orthographic irregularity and nonstandard forms. If there are multiple standard forms, to qualify as irregular, ALL standard forms must be irregular. I exclude verbs marked irregular solely for being defective.)
  3. verb

Language Totals

For those I've "completed" on this page:

  1. 126: English
  2. 70: French
  3. 18: Latin
  4. 11: Japanese
  5. 10: Ancient Greek
  6. 6: Welsh
  7. 4: Polish
